    Wobble
    
    
    A small drawing tool for lines that never sit still. Every stroke is saved as points, and each frame of the loop nudges those points a little. The result is one animated SVG with no scripts, small enough to live on-chain.
    
    
    How it works
    
    
      
      
    • Draw with the pencil and shapes. Fill closed shapes with the bucket.
      • 
      
    • Set how much it wiggles, how fast, and how many frames it loops.
      • 
      
    • Turn on Frame by frame for a layer to draw a flipbook.
      • 
      
    • Keep the meter green, then download, share, or mint.
      • 
    
    
    
    Your drawing
    
    
    Everything stays in your browser. Share links carry the whole drawing inside the URL.
    
    
    After ChainPainter by Brinkman.
